---
name: Website redesign scope
description: Why transactional actions remain on the existing business services.
---
This redesign is a presentation website, not a replacement reservation or commerce system. Preserve the existing official booking, food-ordering, merchandise, and event-inquiry destinations unless the user explicitly requests a migration.

**Why:** The request was to modernize the connected brands' designs; no merchant accounts or reservation integrations were authorized. Locally simulated confirmations would mislead visitors.

**How to apply:** Verify outbound destinations against the official sites when updating transactional links. Concept imagery must be identified as such and not claimed to depict the actual property.
